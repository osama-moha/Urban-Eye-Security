    Ask us on WhatsApp
    Is the site survey free?

    Yes. The initial survey and quotation are free. A formal Risk & Vulnerability Assessment is a separate paid service.

    Can you combine guards, CCTV and alarms?

    Yes. We plan guarding and security systems together around your property.

    How much will it cost?

    It depends on your property and the work required. Your quotation confirms the scope and price.

    Will I pay a monthly fee?

    Some plans include connectivity and support fees. Optional professional monitoring is charged separately.

    Is every installation professionally monitored?

    No. Phone alerts and professional monitoring are different options. We explain both before you decide.

    Do you work outside Nairobi?

    We serve properties across Kenya. Share your location to confirm availability and scheduling.
