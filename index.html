Is the site survey really free?

Yes. We visit your property, check entry points, blind spots, lighting, and the perimeter, and then send you a written quotation. Surveys are scheduled based on your location and how urgent your need is.

What does Urban Eye install and provide?

Smart alarm systems, CCTV installation, access control, fire alarm systems, electric fencing, licensed manned guarding, and 24/7 PRO Monitoring, for homes and businesses in Kenya.

How much does a security system cost?

It depends on the property, so most systems are quoted after the free survey. For reference, smart alarm packages start at KES 59,800 for homes and KES 109,700 for businesses, and the Essential Commercial Fire Package starts at KES 285,000.

Are there monthly fees?

Smart alarm systems need Urban Eye Connect, from KES 1,500/month, to stay online and send alerts. 24/7 PRO Monitoring, with first responders and escalation to armed response when needed, is optional, from KES 8,050/month.

Which areas do you cover?

Our office is on Wabera Street in Nairobi CBD, and we work with properties in Nairobi and across Kenya. Tell us your location when you book and we will confirm when the survey can happen.

Can Urban Eye combine guards, CCTV, and alarms?

Yes. Many properties need more than one layer, such as a guard at the gate, CCTV over entry points, and alarms that alert a response team. The survey recommends the combination your property actually needs.
