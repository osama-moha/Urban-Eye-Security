    Ask us on WhatsApp
    Is the site survey free?

    Yes. The initial site survey and quotation are free. Share your location so we can confirm scheduling. A formal Risk & Vulnerability Assessment is a separate paid service with a written report.

    Can you combine guards, CCTV and alarms?

    Yes. Urban Eye provides guarding alongside security systems. The survey helps define the role of each service at your property.

    How much will it cost?

    The cost depends on the layout, equipment and work required. Package pages provide starting options. Your quotation confirms the proposed scope and price.

    Will I pay a monthly fee?

    Some packages include recurring charges for connectivity and support. Professional monitoring is optional and charged separately. Your quotation explains the charges that apply.

    Is every installation professionally monitored?

    No. Receiving alerts on your phone and having operators review them are different services. We explain both options when preparing your proposal.

    Do you work outside Nairobi?

    Yes. We work with properties across Kenya from our Nairobi office. Share your location so we can confirm scheduling and service availability.
