Is a home program right for you?

A good fit, or better served by something else.

We would rather point you to the right option than sell you a program that does not fit.

A home program is a good fit if

  • You own or rent a house, apartment, or compound in Kenya.
  • You want alerts on your phone, not just a siren.
  • Power cuts or unreliable internet have let an old alarm down.
  • You want to buy your equipment rather than lease it.

You are better served elsewhere if

  • You are protecting a shop, office, or warehouse. See Business Packages
  • You mainly need continuous video of the compound. See CCTV Installation
  • You need a trained person at the gate. See Manned Guarding
  • You only want a siren with no app or monthly connection. These programs are not that product.

Are these final prices?

No. They are starting prices for a typical home of each type. The free site survey confirms how many doors, windows, and outdoor zones need sensors, and you receive a written quotation before anything is installed.

Is the site survey free?

Yes. We visit your home, check entry points, blind spots, and the compound, and then send you a written quotation.

Which program should I choose?

Home Essential suits apartments and smaller homes. Home Verified suits family homes and anyone who travels, because it sends a photo of what triggered the alarm. Home Complete suits large homes and compounds with outdoor areas that need their own sensors.

What will I pay every month?

Every program needs Urban Eye Connect, from KES 1,500/month, to stay online and send alerts. App monitoring costs KES 0/month, and 24/7 PRO Monitoring, with first responders and escalation to armed response when needed, is optional, from KES 8,050/month.

Can I stop Urban Eye Connect and keep my system?

Yes. You buy the hardware outright with no lease obligations, and you can stop the service at any time while keeping your equipment.

Will my pets set off the alarm?

Urban Eye can use pet-immune sensors designed to ignore domestic pets while detecting human movement in protected areas.

Do you have packages for businesses?

Yes. Business packages for shops, offices, warehouses, construction sites, and fire safety are listed on the Business Security Packages page, with shop alarms from KES 91,400.
