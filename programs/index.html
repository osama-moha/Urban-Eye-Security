How is my protection plan confirmed?

The equipment lists are starting configurations. A free survey checks entrances, indoor areas, outdoor approaches and signal coverage. Your written proposal confirms the devices and installation required for your property.

Is the site survey free?

Yes. We visit your home, check entry points, blind spots, and the compound, and then send you a written quotation.

Which program should I choose?

Home Essential suits apartments and smaller homes. Home Verified suits family homes and anyone who travels, because it sends a photo of what triggered the alarm. Home Complete suits large homes and compounds with outdoor areas that need their own sensors.

What will I pay every month?

Connect provides the agreed connectivity, maintenance and remote support. App monitoring means you handle alerts yourself. Operator monitoring is optional and follows a separately agreed service and response plan. All recurring charges are itemised in your quotation.

More questions (3)
Can I stop Urban Eye Connect and keep my system?

Yes. You buy the hardware outright with no lease obligations, and you can stop the service at any time while keeping your equipment.

Will my pets set off the alarm?

Pet-aware detectors can reduce nuisance alarms when chosen and positioned for your animals and room layout. We confirm their limits and settings during the survey.

Do you have packages for businesses?

The warehouse plan is designed around loading bays, stock areas, doors and perimeter risks. Equipment quantities, monitoring and guarding are separately scoped in the written proposal.
