How do you secure loading bays and roller shutters?

It depends on the door type, operating hours, nearby routes, and stock exposure. During the survey we decide whether each bay needs camera coverage, door monitoring, motion detection, lighting, access rules, or a combination.

Can wireless alarms work in a large warehouse?

Wireless range depends on the building, steel, concrete, pallet racking, stock levels, and where devices are placed. We confirm coverage during the survey and test it at installation, and add range extenders where the site needs them.

Will dust set off the fire alarm?

Detector choice depends on stored materials, dust, ventilation and room conditions. The fire design identifies suitable detector types and positions; heavy dust may require a different detection approach.

Can you reduce warehouse shrinkage?

We cannot promise a figure, but we can close the gaps that let it happen. The survey finds weak movement points and gaps in access, detection, and records, and the system gives your team the footage and door logs to investigate discrepancies.

Who responds to alarms after hours?

With optional operator monitoring, supported alerts are reviewed within the contracted hours. Operators follow the agreed contacts and escalation plan; attendance depends on local coverage and the service agreement.

Can guards and technology work together?

Yes. Guards control the gate and vehicles, while cameras, alarms, and fencing cover the bays, stock rooms, and perimeter that one guard cannot watch at the same time. Guarding is quoted separately after the survey.
