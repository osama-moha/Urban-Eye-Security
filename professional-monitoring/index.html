Before you book.

What does Connect pay for?

Connect provides the agreed connectivity, maintenance and remote support. App monitoring means you handle alerts yourself. Operator monitoring is optional and follows a separately agreed service and response plan. All recurring charges are itemised in your quotation.

How quickly will someone attend?

Attendance depends on location, availability and the incident. Your plan confirms coverage and escalation arrangements. A universal arrival time is not promised.

Is armed response included?

Dispatch and any armed-response escalation depend on your agreed plan, local coverage and the incident. We confirm these arrangements before monitoring starts.

Can you use our existing alarm or cameras?

We check equipment compatibility, connectivity and authorised access first. Photo or video verification is available only where supported and configured. Monitoring does not mean continuous viewing of every camera.
