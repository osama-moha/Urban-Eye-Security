When your alarm goes off

What happens between the alert and the response.

Every monitored property follows the same sequence, set out in a response plan you agree before monitoring starts.

01

An alarm is triggered

An intrusion sensor, fire detector, or panic button raises an alert.

02

Two control rooms see it at once

The alert reaches Urban Eye and our response partner's 24/7 control room immediately, so nothing waits for a phone call.

03

The alert is checked

Alarm photos, camera footage where installed, and your site's procedures help separate a real incident from a false alarm.

04

First responders go to site

Confirmed alerts get a response on the ground. If the situation is serious, it is escalated to armed response.

05

You are informed, and it is recorded

Your contacts are updated as agreed, and every event is logged for follow-up and review.

What happens when my alarm goes off?

The alert reaches our team and our response partner's 24/7 control room at the same moment. It is checked using your alarm photos, camera footage where installed, and your site's agreed procedures. If it is real, first responders are sent and your contacts are informed, and the event is recorded.

How fast do responders arrive?

Arrival time varies with your location, the time of day, and traffic, so we do not promise a fixed number of minutes. What does not vary is the alert itself, which reaches our team and our response partner immediately.

Is armed response included?

First responders attend confirmed alerts. When the situation is serious, they escalate it to armed response. Not every alert needs armed officers, and escalating only when it matters keeps armed response available for real threats.

Is there a setup fee?

Yes. A small one-time setup fee covers connecting your system to monitoring and agreeing your response plan. The amount is shown in your written quotation.

What is the difference between PRO Response and PRO Enterprise?

PRO Response suits a single home, shop, or office, and its starting rate covers one primary alarm transmitter. PRO Enterprise suits larger and higher-risk sites that combine fire and intrusion alarms, staff panic buttons, or several buildings or sites. The exact scope is confirmed during your survey.

Can monitoring work with my CCTV?

Yes, when cameras are installed alongside an intrusion or fire alarm. The alarm raises the alert, and camera footage helps the team check what is happening before anyone is sent. We confirm the setup during the survey.

Is response available in my area?

Response coverage depends on where your property is. We confirm it during your free site survey, before you sign up.
