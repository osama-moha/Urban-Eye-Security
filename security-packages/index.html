Are these final prices?

No. They are starting prices for a typical property of each type. The free site survey confirms the doors, sensors, cameras, and detectors your property actually needs, and you receive a written quotation before anything is installed.

Is the site survey free?

Yes. We visit your property, check entry points, blind spots, and the perimeter, and then send you a written quotation.

What does Urban Eye Connect cost, and what does it include?

Urban Eye Connect keeps your system online, sends real-time alerts to your phone, and covers scheduled maintenance and remote technical support. It costs from KES 1,500/month for Essential Retail, KES 2,500/month for Storefront Guardian, and KES 5,000/month for Office Shield Connect Pro.

Do I need 24/7 PRO Monitoring?

It is optional. PRO Monitoring, from KES 8,050/month, adds 24/7 checking of alerts, first responders for confirmed incidents, and escalation to armed response when needed. It suits premises where nobody can reliably respond at night, such as a shop after closing.

Should I buy or lease security for a construction site?

Buy the equipment, from KES 225,000, if you will manage the building afterwards or reuse the equipment on your next project. Lease it, from KES 38,500/month with setup from KES 35,000, if you want Urban Eye to supply, install, and maintain the system for the length of the project.

Why is there no fixed price for warehouses?

Warehouses vary too much in size, loading bays, and stock value for a fixed package to be accurate, so every warehouse receives a written quotation after the free survey. PRO Enterprise Monitoring starts at KES 10,500/month, and guarding is quoted separately.

Do you have packages for homes?

Yes. Home packages start at KES 59,800 and are listed on the Home Programs page.
