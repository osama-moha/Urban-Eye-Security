Before you book.

Are these final prices?

Published equipment and package prices are starting points and exclude VAT. Your survey confirms installation, additions, software and recurring charges before you approve.

Is the survey free?

Yes. The initial site survey and quotation are free. A diagnostic review of an existing faulty system or a formal risk assessment is separately scoped and priced.

What will I pay each month?

Retail alarm Connect starts from KES 1,500/month for Essential Retail and KES 2,500/month for Storefront Guardian. Monitoring is optional unless included in the selected lease. Office access licences and support are separately scoped.

Should we buy or lease site equipment?

Purchase suits equipment you want to own and reuse. The Managed Site Lease starts from KES 38,500/month plus setup from KES 35,000. The proposal confirms duration, included services and the 50% setup credit on contracts of six months or more.
