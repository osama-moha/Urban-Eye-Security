Before you book.

Are these final prices?

The figures are guide or starting prices and exclude VAT. Your written quotation confirms equipment, installation scope, additions and ongoing charges. Office and warehouse plans are quoted after a survey.

Is the site survey free?

Yes. The initial survey and quotation are free. Diagnosing an existing faulty system or preparing a formal risk assessment is a separately scoped paid service.

What is the difference between Connect and monitoring?

Connect provides the agreed alarm connection, maintenance and remote support. Optional operator monitoring adds alert review and an agreed escalation plan. It is included only where your selected package or lease explicitly says so.

Can you combine systems and guarding?

Yes. The site plan can combine access control, cameras, alarms and guarding. The quotation identifies what is included, who handles alerts and any separate service charges.
