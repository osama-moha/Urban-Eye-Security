When someone tries the fence

What happens between the wire and your phone.

This is where our fences differ. Most stop at a siren on site. Ours carry on to your phone.

01

A pulse runs along the wire

A Nemtek energizer sends a short, high-voltage pulse that is painful but not lethal, so climbing the fence hurts.

02

The energizer watches the wire

If the wire is cut, shorted, or tampered with, the energizer raises an alarm, on larger properties for the exact zone.

03

The alarm sounds on site

A siren warns the intruder, and anyone on the property, that the fence has been touched.

04

The alert reaches your phone

Because the fence is connected to an Ajax alarm, the alert appears in the Ajax app wherever you are.

05

Someone acts on it

You decide what to do, or with PRO Monitoring, our team and our response partner see the alert at the same moment and follow your response plan.

The free survey

We check the weak points before quoting the fence.

A fence is only as strong as the gaps around it, so the survey looks beyond the wall itself.

Wall Condition and Height

Whether your wall can carry wall-top brackets, or whether a free-standing fence is the better option.

Hidden Ways In

Trees and vegetation near the fence, storm drains, pipe routes, and service openings that let someone get past the wire.

Gates and Zones

Where the gates are, how long the boundary is, and whether splitting it into zones will help you find a breach quickly.

Power, Alerts, and Cameras

Where the energizer and backup battery go, how the fence connects to your Ajax alarm, and whether cameras would help cover the boundary.

Is an electric fence right for you?

A good fit, or better served by something else.

A fence stops people at the boundary. Sometimes you need something that also shows who they were or stops them at the door.

An electric fence is a good fit if

  • You have a wall or boundary that intruders can climb.
  • You want to know on your phone when someone tries.
  • You are often away, or the property is empty at night.
  • You want to stop intruders before they reach the house or building.

You are better served elsewhere if

  • You need to see who tried to get in. See CCTV Installation
  • You rent and cannot alter the wall. See Home Programs
  • You need someone to act on alarms at night. See PRO Monitoring

How much does an electric fence cost?

Electric fencing is quoted per metre after a free site survey. The price depends on the length of the boundary, whether the fence goes on top of a wall or stands on its own, the number of zones and gates, and the backup battery your property needs. You receive a written quotation before any work starts.

Is an electric fence dangerous?

The fence delivers a short, high-voltage pulse that is painful but not lethal. The aim is to make climbing the wall a bad idea, not to injure anyone.

Will I get an alert on my phone?

Yes. Most installers stop at a siren on site. We connect the fence to an Ajax alarm system, so when the fence alarm triggers, the alert reaches the Ajax app on your phone wherever you are.

Does the fence keep working during a power cut?

Yes. A backup battery keeps the energizer running when the power goes off. The battery is sized to your property during the survey.

Can you tell which part of the fence was touched?

On properties large enough to need it, the fence is split into zones, so the alarm shows which section was touched. The number of zones depends on the size of the property.

Which energizers do you install?

We install Nemtek energizers.

Can the fence work with monitoring and cameras?

Yes. Because the fence connects to an Ajax alarm, it can be added to PRO Monitoring, where alerts reach our team and our response partner at the same moment. CCTV can be added where you also need to see who tried to get in.
