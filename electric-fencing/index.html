What determines your protection plan

The survey measures your boundary and checks its condition, so the written quotation reflects the fence your property actually needs.

  • Length of the boundary Priced per metre of fence.
  • Wall-top or free-standing A fence on an existing wall needs less material than one built from the ground.
  • Zones, gates, and corners More zones and openings mean more wiring and hardware.
  • Backup battery Sized to keep your energizer running through power cuts.

Before you book.

What determines the price?

Boundary length, wall condition, fence type, gates, zones and power requirements determine the quotation. We measure and inspect the site first.

Will alarms reach my phone?

Phone alerts require a compatible, configured interface and working communications. The survey confirms equipment and any ongoing connection charges.

Will it work during a power cut?

A specified backup battery can support the energizer during a power cut. Runtime depends on the battery, load and condition; we confirm the design during the survey.

What maintenance is needed?

Keep vegetation clear and arrange checks of wires, insulators, earthing, alarms and backup power. We explain access and maintenance precautions at handover.
