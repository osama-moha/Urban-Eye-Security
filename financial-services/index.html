How do silent panic buttons avoid false alarms?

For fobs we use the Ajax DoubleButton, which only raises an alarm when both of its buttons are pressed at the same time. The buttons are firm and separated by a divider, so a single accidental press does nothing.

Where does a panic alert go?

With PRO Monitoring, the alert reaches our team and our response partner's 24/7 control room immediately, without a siren sounding in the branch. First responders attend, serious incidents are escalated to armed response, and the branch contacts in your response plan are informed.

Can you detect an attack on a safe or strongroom?

Yes. Wired Ajax Superior detectors on the safe and strongroom door raise an alarm on shock, tilt, or opening. If your insurer or specification calls for certified detection on vault walls, we specify it during the branch risk assessment and source it for your site.

Can two people be required to open the strongroom?

Yes. Suprema BioStar 2 can require two authorised people to present their credentials before a door opens. For paired doors, an interlock keeps the second door locked while the first is open, so only one door of the pair opens at a time.

Can we see every branch from head office?

Yes. Alarm events from every branch can be followed in one Ajax account, and door access for all branches is managed from a central Suprema BioStar 2 system.

Do you install outside trading hours?

That is how we normally work, so customers and daily cash handling are not disturbed. The installation dates are agreed with you in the written proposal.

What does the branch risk assessment cost?

The fee depends on the size and number of branches and is confirmed in writing before the assessment is scheduled. It is credited in full if you go ahead with an Urban Eye installation within 90 days of the report.
