Plan each control around a branch task.

Counters and staff safety

Position discreet panic devices where staff can reach them. Agree who receives the alert and what they should do.

Staff-only doors

Specify readers, locks, exit hardware and permissions for selected rooms. Two-person approval and interlocking require a supported system and door design.

Entrances and teller areas

Select camera positions, lenses and storage for the views you need. Recording retention is a design target confirmed in the quotation.

Safes and strongrooms

Assess the safe, door and surrounding construction before selecting detection. Opening and shock sensors do not establish specialist vault coverage.

Agree what happens after a panic alert.

  1. 01
    Configure the device. Select a panic button or compatible input and test the intended silent operation with authorised staff.
  2. 02
    Set the contacts. Record the responsible people, supported alerts and escalation instructions.
  3. 03
    Confirm attendance coverage. Optional operators follow the contracted hours and response arrangements. Dispatch and escalation depend on the plan, location and incident.

Before you book.

Do we need a risk assessment or a free survey?

A paid risk assessment produces a written review and prioritised recommendations. A free site survey scopes proposed installation work. We confirm which visit fits your request before booking.

Can head office manage several branches?

We check the selected alarm and access platforms, connectivity and administrator permissions. Supported multi-site functions and licences are listed in the proposal.

Is armed response included?

Attendance and any armed-response escalation depend on the agreed monitoring plan, local coverage and incident. Confirm these arrangements before service starts.

How long are camera recordings kept?

Storage is sized for the agreed retention target. Camera count, resolution, recording schedule and scene activity affect the available recording period.
