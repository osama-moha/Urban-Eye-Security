FAQs

Common financial security questions.

Key questions regarding branch compliance, vault sensor engineering, and silent duress workflows.

How do seismic sensors detect safe drilling or cutting before a breach?

Seismic sensors measure acoustic vibrations and thermal changes caused by drilling, diamond cutting, torches, or heavy impacts against safe walls. They trigger alarm alerts immediately, before physical penetration occurs.

How do silent panic and duress buttons work during business hours?

Concealed under-counter buttons and wireless foot pedals allow staff to trigger silent alarms without audible sirens. The alert routes instantly to the central monitoring station with top priority for emergency verification.

Can multiple branch locations be monitored under a single management system?

Yes. Security managers can oversee branch opening and closing timestamps, review access logs, and receive real-time incident notifications across multiple sites through a unified interface.
