Is guarding right for you?

A good fit, or better served by something else.

Guards are the right answer when a person needs to be present. Sometimes technology covers the gap for less.

Guarding is a good fit if

  • You need a person at a gate, reception, or yard during set hours.
  • Visitors, deliveries, or contractors need checking before they enter.
  • You want written instructions and records, not just someone in uniform.
  • You want guards backed by cameras, alarms, or monitoring.

You are better served elsewhere if

  • You mainly need to know if someone breaks in at night. See Smart Alarm Systems
  • You need footage of what happened. See CCTV Installation
  • You need someone to act on alarms at an empty property. See PRO Monitoring
  • You want the cheapest guard with no instructions or supervision. This service is not that.

How much do security guards cost?

Guarding is quoted per site after a free site survey. The price depends on how many posts your site needs, the hours each post is covered, and what the guards must control. You receive a written quotation before any guard is deployed.

Do you provide guards for both estates and offices?

Yes. We provide guarding for private homes, apartments, estates, offices, shops, warehouses, and other properties. Post instructions and visitor procedures are written for the needs of each site.

What does the site survey cover?

The survey is free. We review your property layout, perimeter, blind spots, lighting, existing alarms and cameras, access routines, and visitor movement, then work out how many posts and which procedures the site needs.

What happens if a guard faces a serious incident?

The incident follows the escalation contacts agreed for your site. Where the site also has PRO Monitoring, alerts reach our team and our response partner at the same moment, first responders attend, and serious incidents are escalated to armed response.

How do guards work with CCTV and alarms?

Cameras and alarms cover the areas a guard cannot watch at the same time, such as a rear fence while the guard is at the gate. Where they are installed, alerts can be shared with the guard on site so they know where to look.

Is Urban Eye licensed to provide guards?

Yes. Urban Eye is registered with the Private Security Regulatory Authority (PSRA) and is a member of the PSIA.
