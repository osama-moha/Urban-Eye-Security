Protection plan

Guarding is quoted per site, after a free survey.

Two properties of the same size can need very different guarding, so a fixed price would be wrong for most of them.

What determines your protection plan

The survey works out what your property actually needs. You then receive a written quotation showing the posts, hours, and any added monitoring before a single guard is deployed.

  • Number of posts A gate, a reception desk, a patrol route, or several of them.
  • Hours covered Daytime, overnight, or around the clock.
  • What guards must control Visitors, deliveries, stock, cash, or contractor access.
  • Technology alongside the guards CCTV, alarms, or PRO Monitoring added to cover what guards cannot.

Before you book.

How is guarding priced?

The quotation depends on posts, hours, duties and supervision. Equipment, monitoring and other services are listed separately where required.

Can you cover day and night shifts?

Shift coverage is scoped for your property. The proposal confirms hours, staffing and handover arrangements before deployment.

What happens during an incident?

Guards follow the agreed post instructions and escalation contacts. Emergency services or response partners are contacted as the situation and service arrangements require.

Can guards work with cameras and alarms?

Yes. We agree who reviews available alerts or footage and what action guards should take. Monitoring and response coverage are separately confirmed.
