Before you book.

How is the plan quoted?

The survey confirms controlled doors, credential choices and software requirements. Your quotation lists equipment, installation, licences and any recurring charges.

Can each team have different access?

Yes. Permissions can be set for selected rooms, floors and working hours. Multi-site administration and any lift controls are scoped for the selected platform.

How do we remove staff access?

An authorised administrator disables the credential in the selected platform. Updates must reach the relevant devices; we confirm how changes are managed across your doors and sites.

What about power cuts and emergency exits?

Lock type, backup power, exit hardware and any fire-alarm interface are assessed together. The agreed operation is tested during commissioning.
