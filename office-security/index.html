What happens when an employee leaves?

You cancel their card, code, or fingerprint in the software, and it stops working at every door straight away. There are no keys to collect and no locks to change.

Can I arm individual rooms?

Yes. The alarm is split into zones, so the server room, finance office, or records room can stay armed while the open office is in use.

Can I limit visitor access?

Yes. On Suprema systems, reception issues a card or QR code pass that opens only the doors and lift floors assigned to the visitor, for the period of the visit.

More questions (3)
What about biometric access?

It can be, if it is set up properly. Kenya's Data Protection Act treats biometric data as sensitive personal data, so plan for staff consent, a data protection impact assessment, and a card option for anyone who declines. This is a summary, not legal advice.

What happens to the doors in a fire?

Door release depends on the lock, escape route and configured fire interface. We assess the doors during the survey and test the agreed operation at commissioning. The fire package starts from KES 285,000; the survey confirms the integration scope.

Do we need 24/7 monitoring?

It is optional. PRO Monitoring starts from KES 8,050/month. Operators review supported after-hours and panic alerts according to the coverage, contacts and response arrangements agreed for your site.
