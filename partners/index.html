Reporting

Agree the records, reporting frequency and recipients. Portfolio summaries are included only where specified.

Adding properties

Survey each new site and approve its equipment, installation and recurring costs before adding it to the agreement.

Contacts and escalation

Name the responsible contacts, support channels and incident escalation procedure for covered services.

Maintenance and scheduling

Agree visit scope, booking arrangements and service hours. Any response or attendance commitment belongs in the service agreement.

Start with one property.

  1. Share the portfolio. Tell us the locations, existing equipment, services and operational concerns.
  2. Agree the first visit. A new-work survey is free. A formal written assessment or technical diagnosis is scoped and priced separately.
  3. Review the proposal. Approve equipment, responsibilities, reporting and ongoing charges for the initial site.
  4. Expand by site. Use the agreed process to review additional properties, with their own requirements and costs.
