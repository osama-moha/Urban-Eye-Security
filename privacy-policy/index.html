Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Urban Eye Security ("Urban Eye," "we," "us") of St. Ellis, Wabera Street, Nairobi CBD, Kenya, is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. It explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

This policy covers:

  • Visits to this website
  • Enquiries you send us through a form, WhatsApp, phone, or email
  • Personal data handled while we install, monitor, or guard a property
  • Job applications

Enquiries and site survey requests

When you book a free site survey, request a risk audit, or contact us, we collect:

  • Your name and WhatsApp number
  • Your company name, for business requests
  • Your property type, location, main security concern, and, where you choose to share them, your timeframe and site size
  • The page or service you asked about
  • Anything else you tell us in your messages, calls, or emails

We use these details to reply to you, usually on WhatsApp, to arrange the site survey, and to prepare a written quotation. We do not sell your personal data.

Website analytics and cookies

This website uses Google Analytics and Google Ads to count visits, see which pages and enquiry forms are used, and measure whether our advertising works. When you first visit, a cookie notice asks you to accept or decline.

  • Before you choose, or if you decline: analytics and advertising cookies are not set. Google's tags still send basic signals without cookies, such as that a page was viewed, a form was sent, or a WhatsApp or phone link was tapped, along with browser and device type. Ad personalisation stays off.
  • If you accept: Google Analytics and Google Ads set cookies so visits and advertising results can be measured more accurately.

We never send your name or phone number to Google Analytics. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

This site is hosted by Vercel, whose servers record technical details such as IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Security systems and services we deliver

Depending on the service you engage us for, the systems we install, monitor, or operate may handle personal data about you, your staff, residents, guests, and visitors, such as:

  • CCTV recordings and camera snapshots
  • Alarm, arming, and monitoring event records
  • Access control records, including cards, codes, and entry times
  • Fingerprints or face templates, where biometric access control is used
  • Visitor, delivery, and gate records kept by guards
  • Names and phone numbers of the contact people for a site

The written service agreement for each engagement sets out who controls this data, who can access it, and how long it is kept. We handle it only to deliver the service you engaged us for.

Kenyan law treats fingerprints and face templates as sensitive personal data. Where biometric access control is used, the people enrolled should be told and give consent, and a card or code option should be available for anyone who declines.

With PRO Monitoring, when an alarm is triggered, the alert details reach our team and our response partner at the same time. These details include the site location, the type of event, and the site contacts. Our response partner uses them only to attend and respond to the incident.

Job applications

If you apply for a role by email, we receive the documents you send. These may include your application letter, ID or passport copy, certificates, Certificate of Good Conduct, and referee details. We use them only to assess your application and check your suitability for the role. We keep them only for as long as recruitment for that role requires.

Why we are allowed to use your data

  • Your request: replying to an enquiry and preparing a survey or quotation you asked for.
  • A contract with you: delivering installation, monitoring, and guarding services.
  • Your consent: analytics and advertising cookies, and biometric enrolment.
  • Our legitimate interests: keeping this website secure and improving our services.
  • Legal obligations: for example, tax records or lawful requests from authorities.

Who we share your data with

  • Formspree, which delivers website form submissions to our team.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), when we message you or you message us.
  • Google, for analytics, advertising measurement, and website fonts.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website.
  • Our response partner, for monitored alarms, as described above.
  • Installation or maintenance subcontractors, only as needed to deliver your service.
  • Police or other authorities, where the law requires it or you ask us to report an incident.

International data transfers

Formspree, Google, Meta, and Vercel may process data on servers outside Kenya. Where personal data is transferred outside Kenya, we rely on providers that maintain appropriate safeguards for personal data, in line with section 48 of the Data Protection Act, 2019.

How long we keep data

  • Enquiries: if an enquiry does not lead to an engagement, we delete or anonymise it within 12 months of your last contact with us.
  • Customers: customer and service data is kept for the period set out in the service agreement, and longer only where the law requires it.
  • Analytics: analytics data is kept according to our Google Analytics retention settings.

How we protect data

Access to enquiry, customer, and system data is limited to the people who need it to do their work. We use accounts and devices with access controls for the systems we manage.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Be told how your personal data is used
  • Access your personal data
  • Correct or delete it
  • Object to or restrict how it is used
  • Ask for it in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent you have given, including for cookies

To make a request, contact us using the details below.

You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) if you believe your personal data has been mishandled.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
