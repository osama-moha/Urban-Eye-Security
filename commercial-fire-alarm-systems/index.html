Before you book.

How much detection does my building need?

The survey determines detector type, quantity, positions, warning devices, backup power and any building interfaces. Three detectors are an example configuration, not a coverage allowance for every building.

Does installation guarantee an audit pass?

No. Fire safety also depends on escape routes, procedures, training, maintenance and other measures. The alarm installation has a defined scope and does not certify the entire building.

Can it release doors or stop ventilation?

Compatible interfaces can be designed for agreed functions. Door hardware, building systems and the cause-and-effect requirements must be assessed and tested together.

What happens if power or internet fails?

Local operation depends on the specified backup power and system design. Remote alerts need an available communication path. The survey and commissioning tests confirm the agreed arrangements.
