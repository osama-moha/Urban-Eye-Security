What the law requires

Five workplace fire duties every Kenyan business owner should know.

These come from the Occupational Safety and Health Act, 2007 and the Fire Risk Reduction Rules, 2007. Contravening the Rules is an offence.

Provide and maintain fire detection

Every occupier must provide and maintain fire detection appliances, placed to raise the alarm immediately and connected to audible and visual warning devices (Rule 28). The package covers this with detectors, a call point, and a siren with strobe.

Have it inspected every 12 months

Fire detection appliances must be regularly maintained and inspected at least once every twelve months by a competent person (Rule 28). Your commissioning record and device list give the inspector a starting point.

Complete a yearly fire safety audit

Every occupier must have a fire safety audit carried out at least once every twelve months by an approved fire safety auditor, pay for it, and keep the report (Rule 36). The audit is independent; our role is to make sure your detection and alarm system is ready for it.

Keep escape routes open and practise evacuation

Means of escape must be kept free from obstruction, and evacuation procedures must be tested at regular intervals (OSHA 2007, sections 81 and 82). Automatic door release keeps locked exits from blocking the route.

Report any fire within 24 hours

Any fire at a workplace must be reported to the nearest occupational safety and health area office within 24 hours, followed by a written report within seven days (Rule 35). The hub's event log shows what triggered and when.

Technical proof

The technology behind the alarm.

We install the Ajax EN54 fire system: certified to the European fire detection standard and built for buildings where power and internet are not guaranteed.

Certified to EN 54

The hub is certified to EN 54-2, 4, 21, and 25, covering control and indicating equipment, power supply, alarm transmission, and wireless links. Across the system, the certification also covers sounders, heat and smoke detectors, manual call points, input and output modules, and visual alarm devices.

Smoke Detection That Ignores Steam

Each detector uses blue and infrared light to tell real smoke apart from steam and vapour, has a dust-protected smoke chamber, and carries two thermistors for heat detection.

Four Ways to Stay Connected

The hub connects over Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and two SIM cards on 4G, and runs for up to 24 hours on its backup battery during a power cut.

Wireless, With Room to Grow

One hub supports up to 200 devices, with a radio range of up to 1,800 metres in open space and range extenders for larger buildings. Adding a detector later does not mean new cabling.

Is a fire alarm a legal requirement for my business?

For workplaces, yes. The Fire Risk Reduction Rules, 2007 require every occupier to provide and maintain fire detection appliances, connect them to audible and visual warning devices, and have them inspected at least once every twelve months by a competent person. Contravening the Rules is an offence. This is a summary, not legal advice, so confirm your specific obligations with your fire safety auditor or the Directorate of Occupational Safety and Health Services.

Will installing this system mean I pass my fire safety audit?

No installer can promise that. The yearly fire safety audit is carried out by an approved fire safety auditor and covers more than detection, including escape routes, firefighting equipment, training, and your fire safety policy. What we give you is a working, tested system and the handover documents an auditor asks to see. Your survey can also add signage, emergency lighting, extinguishers, and fire marshal training.

Will cooking, steam, or dust set off false alarms?

The smoke detectors use a dual-spectrum optical sensor with blue and infrared light to tell real smoke apart from steam and vapour, and the smoke chamber is protected against dust. During the survey we also decide where a heat detector is the better choice, such as directly above cooking equipment.

What happens during a power cut or internet outage?

The hub runs on its backup battery for up to 24 hours without mains power. It connects over Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and two SIM cards on 4G, so if one connection drops, alerts continue through another.

Can the fire alarm unlock doors and shut down air conditioning?

Yes. The package includes an automation module that we connect to your fail-safe door locks and HVAC system. When the fire alarm activates, doors on the escape route release and air handling shuts down so it does not spread smoke. Larger buildings can add modules for lift recall and ventilation shutdown.

Do you need to run cables through the whole building?

No. Detectors, sirens, and call points communicate wirelessly with the hub, so there are no cable runs between devices and no ceilings to open up. The hub needs a power connection, and automation modules are wired to the door locks or equipment they control.
