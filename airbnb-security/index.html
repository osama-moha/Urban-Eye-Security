Airbnb's camera rules

Security that keeps your listing on the right side of Airbnb.

Airbnb tightened its rules on cameras and devices in 2024. We plan every host setup around them, so your security never becomes the reason for a guest complaint.

No Cameras Inside the Listing

Since 30 April 2024, Airbnb does not allow security cameras inside listings, whatever their location, purpose, or disclosure. We use door sensors, standard motion sensors, and smoke and leak detectors indoors instead.

Outdoor Cameras Must Be Disclosed

Outdoor cameras are allowed if you disclose their presence and general location before guests book. They must not look into indoor spaces or private outdoor areas such as an outdoor shower or sauna.

Noise Monitors Are Allowed in Common Areas

Airbnb allows disclosed noise decibel monitors in common spaces. They measure sound levels only and do not record conversations.

This is a summary of Airbnb's policy as published. Airbnb can change its rules, so check Airbnb's Help Centre before you list.

Can I install cameras inside an Airbnb?

No. Since 30 April 2024, Airbnb does not allow security cameras inside listings, whatever their location, purpose, or disclosure. Outdoor cameras are allowed if you disclose their presence and general location before guests book, as long as they do not look into indoor spaces or private outdoor areas such as an outdoor shower or sauna.

Can each guest get their own access code?

Yes. A smart keypad lets you create a code for each stay and delete it when the guest checks out, so past guests cannot get back in and there are no keys to copy.

Does a motion detector that takes photos count as a camera?

A detector that captures images indoors could fall under Airbnb's ban on indoor cameras, so for listings we use standard motion sensors inside and keep any image-capturing devices outdoors.

Can the system protect the unit between bookings?

Yes. Between stays you arm the alarm from your phone, and door and motion sensors, smoke detectors, and leak detectors alert you the moment something happens, wherever you are.

Home Essential, a wireless alarm for apartments and smaller homes, starts at KES 59,800, plus Urban Eye Connect from KES 1,500/month. Keyless access, outdoor cameras, and smoke and leak detectors are quoted after a free site survey, because every property is different.

Do I need 24/7 monitoring?

It is optional. It suits hosts who are far from the property. With PRO Monitoring, from KES 8,050/month, alerts reach our team and our response partner at the same moment, first responders attend, and serious incidents are escalated to armed response.

I manage several listings. Can I see them all in one place?

Yes. Each property's alarm hub can be added to the same Ajax app account, so you can arm, disarm, and check alerts for every listing from one phone.
