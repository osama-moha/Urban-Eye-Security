Airbnb & Short-Stay Security

A security checklist for short-stay hosts

Plan guest access, non-camera indoor detection, exterior cameras and empty-property alerts.

Urban Eye Security Updated 22 October 2026 2 min read
Back to Insights

Plan separately for guest access, occupied stays and empty periods. Give each device a purpose and name the person responsible for alerts.

Before choosing devices

Airbnb prohibits cameras monitoring indoor spaces, including cameras that are switched off. Exterior cameras must meet its placement and disclosure rules. Check the current Airbnb device policy before installation or listing changes.

Use non-camera indoor alarm detectors for our rental configurations. Treat indoor photo detectors as image-capturing devices, not as an exception to the camera rules.

Before each arrival

  • Create supported guest credentials and check their expiry.
  • Confirm who holds any physical override key.
  • Check that the listing describes exterior devices accurately.
  • Give guests access instructions and the relevant help contacts.

During the stay

Keep the agreed fire and leak detection active. Choose the devices for the required function and property conditions. Agree how the host or caretaker will handle those alerts.

Keep exterior camera views within permitted areas. An installation quotation does not replace the host’s responsibility to maintain an accurate listing.

After checkout

  • Revoke guest credentials and check caretaker permissions.
  • Check doors, windows and any physical keys.
  • Arm the agreed empty-property detection.
  • Review faults and confirm who can attend if an alert arrives.

If you manage several properties

Keep separate administrator permissions, device records and contact instructions for each property. Multi-property app access and remote lock management depend on the selected platforms.
