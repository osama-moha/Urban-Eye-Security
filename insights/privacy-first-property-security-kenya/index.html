Privacy & Security Planning

Plan cameras and access records around a clear purpose

A practical checklist for camera views, access records, permissions and retention in Kenyan properties.

Urban Eye Security Updated 22 October 2026 2 min read
Back to Insights

Before approving cameras or access control, record what each system is for, who manages it and what happens to the information it collects.

Define the security task

Identify the entrance, stock movement or restricted door you need to review. Public-area surveillance does not define the coverage or operating procedures needed at your own property.

Ask the installer to explain each proposed view and access record. Remove coverage that has no agreed purpose.

Record the data decisions

  • Which cameras or readers collect identifiable information?
  • Who owns the system and controls administrator access?
  • Who can view, export or share records?
  • How long should recordings and access logs be retained?
  • Where is data stored, including any cloud service?
  • How are requests, incidents and account changes handled?

Check visitors and staff separately

Document which visitor details are needed and how they are handled. Do not assume every site needs ID copies or biometric credentials. Ask the responsible privacy adviser to confirm the appropriate process for your organisation.

Review administrator access when staff, contractors or providers change. Keep credentials and configuration records in the agreed handover process.

Confirm requirements before implementation

Review the ODPC’s data protection law resources and obtain advice for your actual processing activities. This checklist is planning guidance and does not certify compliance.

The ODPC private-security guidance document is labelled as a draft. Confirm the current applicable guidance before relying on it.

Bring these decisions into the quotation

Include camera positions, storage, authorised access, handover records and any ongoing services in the scope. Explore CCTV design or door access control.
