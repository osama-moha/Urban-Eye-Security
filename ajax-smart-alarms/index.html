Intelligent Detection Capabilities

Built for verified clarity, not false alarm panic.

The strongest alarm system is not the loudest one. It is the system that confirms the threat with visual proof and coordinates immediate action.

9-Second Visual Verification

MotionCam sensors transmit a series of high-definition photos the instant movement occurs. Both you and our central control room see the intruder immediately, eliminating false alarms and authorizing immediate armed dispatch.

Grade 2 Encrypted Wireless

Operating on proprietary Jeweller radio technology with two-way encrypted handshakes, multi-frequency hopping against radio jamming, and up to 2,000 meters of open-air transmission without invasive wall cabling.

Blackout & Sabotage Resilience

Integrated lithium backup power provides up to 16 hours of independent operation during KPLC grid failure. Dual SIM slots (Safaricom and Airtel) switch data channels automatically if fixed broadband drops.

Digital Access & Smartphone Control

Arm, disarm, bypass individual zones, check device battery levels, and grant scheduled access to staff or cleaning crews directly from an intuitive, encrypted mobile application.
