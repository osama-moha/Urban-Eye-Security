When your alarm goes off

What happens between the trigger and the response.

Every system follows the same verified sequence, whether you monitor alerts yourself or hand them to Urban Eye PRO Monitoring.

01

A sensor trips

A door contact, motion detector, or glass-break sensor detects entry, and the hub raises an alert immediately.

02

The alert reaches your phone

You get a push notification straight away. Photo-verified detectors attach a picture, so you can see what triggered it.

03

The threat is verified

On the free app plan, you check the alert and decide. With PRO Monitoring, our team and our response partner see it at the same moment and check it.

04

Response is coordinated

A confirmed threat activates your agreed response plan. With PRO Monitoring, first responders attend, and serious incidents are escalated to armed response.

05

Everything is recorded

Each event is logged in your system history, giving you a clear record of what happened and what was done.

Is a smart alarm right for you?

A good fit, or better served by something else.

We would rather point you to the right system than sell you the wrong one.

A smart alarm is a good fit if

  • You want to know an alert is real before you or anyone else responds.
  • Your property has clear entry points: doors, windows, a shop front, or an office suite.
  • Power cuts or unreliable internet have let your old alarm down.
  • You want to own your equipment instead of leasing it.

You are better served elsewhere if

  • You need continuous video of an open compound or yard. See CCTV Installation
  • You are protecting a long perimeter wall or open land. See Electric Fencing
  • You need a trained person on site at all times. See Manned Guarding
  • You only want a siren, with no app or monthly connection. A smart alarm is not the right product.

What will I pay every month?

Every system needs Urban Eye Connect to stay online and send alerts. It starts from KES 1,500/month for homes and small shops, KES 2,500/month for Storefront Guardian, and KES 5,000/month for Office Shield. 24/7 PRO Monitoring, with first responders and escalation to armed response when needed, is optional, from KES 8,050/month.

Can I stop Urban Eye Connect and keep my system?

Yes. You own the hardware outright with no lease obligations, and you can stop the service at any time while keeping your equipment.

Do I need PRO Monitoring?

No. App monitoring costs KES 0/month: you receive alerts and decide what to do. PRO Monitoring suits properties where nobody can reliably respond, such as a shop at night or a home while you travel.

Will my pets set off the alarm?

No. Urban Eye can use pet-immune sensors designed to ignore domestic pets while detecting human movement in protected areas.

Is a wireless alarm reliable for a large home or business?

Yes. Smart alarm systems provide secure encrypted wireless communication with long-range coverage suitable for many homes and commercial spaces, with range-extension options for larger sites.

What happens if the power or internet goes out?

Smart alarm hubs include backup power and can use multiple communication channels depending on the model and setup, helping alerts continue during internet or power interruptions.

Can the alarm system work with physical guards or response teams?

Yes. Smart alarm alerts can support guarding, monitoring, and response procedures by showing what triggered the alarm and where action is needed.
