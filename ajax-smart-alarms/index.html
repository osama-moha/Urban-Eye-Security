Do I need a paid health check?

No. If you are installing a new alarm system and know the property you want to protect, Urban Eye can carry out a free commercial site survey and provide a scoped quotation. The paid Health Check is for existing, faulty, inherited, poorly documented, or unreliable alarm systems where technical diagnosis is required before recommending the right work.

What are the monthly fees?

Connect provides the agreed connectivity, maintenance and remote support. App monitoring means you handle alerts yourself. Operator monitoring is optional and follows a separately agreed service and response plan. All recurring charges are itemised in your quotation.

Can I cancel Connect and keep the equipment?

Yes. You own the hardware outright with no lease obligations, and you can stop the service at any time while keeping your equipment.

Do I need PRO Monitoring?

No. App monitoring has no operator-monitoring fee, but the package’s required Connect service still applies. PRO Monitoring is optional and follows an agreed monitoring and response plan.

More questions (4)
Will my pets set off the alarm?

Pet-aware detectors can reduce nuisance alarms when selected and positioned for your pets and property. We confirm the detector type and settings during the survey.

Will wireless cover my property?

Yes. Smart alarm systems provide secure encrypted wireless communication with long-range coverage suitable for many homes and commercial spaces, with range-extension options for larger sites.

What happens if the power or internet goes out?

Smart alarm hubs include backup power and can use multiple communication channels depending on the model and setup, helping alerts continue during internet or power interruptions.

Can alarms work with guards?

Yes. Smart alarm alerts can support guarding, monitoring, and response procedures by showing what triggered the alarm and where action is needed.
