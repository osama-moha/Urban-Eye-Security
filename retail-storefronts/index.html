After you lock up

What happens when someone tries your shop at night.

The same sequence runs whether you handle alerts yourself or hand them to PRO Monitoring.

01

A sensor trips

A detector at a protected opening or indoor area triggers an alarm and the configured siren.

02

Your phone gets the alert

An alarm alert identifies the triggered detector. Photo packages also provide images from the selected detector area.

03

The alert is checked

You look at the photo and decide, or with PRO Monitoring our team and our response partner check it for you.

04

Someone responds

With PRO Monitoring, first responders attend confirmed alerts, and serious incidents are escalated to armed response.

05

You have the evidence

The alarm event is logged, and your cameras hold the recording to share with the police or your insurer.

Is this right for your shop?

A good fit, or better served by something else.

We would rather point you to the right option than sell you a package your shop does not need.

A shop package is a good fit if

  • Your shop is empty at night and the stock is worth stealing.
  • You want to know an alarm is real before you drive over.
  • Stock or cash goes missing and you need footage to find out how.
  • Your staff handle cash and need a way to call for help.

You are better served elsewhere if

  • You run an office rather than a shop. See Office Security
  • You need a guard at the door during trading hours. See Manned Guarding
  • You are protecting a home. See Home Programs

How much does shop security cost?

Business Essential covers selected openings and indoor motion. Business Photo adds indoor alarm photographs. Business Outdoor adds detection at a selected outdoor approach. A free site survey confirms coverage before a written quotation separates equipment, installation, VAT and recurring charges.

What will I pay every month?

Business alarm packages have a required Connect service quoted for the agreed connectivity, maintenance and remote support. Optional operator monitoring has separate setup, coverage and response terms. Your quotation confirms all recurring charges.

Can we restrict which staff members can arm or disarm the shop?

Yes. You can give managers and staff their own codes or tags, restrict sensitive areas, and review who opened or closed the shop.

How do you prevent false alarms when staff arrive in the morning?

Urban Eye sets entry delays, staff codes or tags, and an opening routine, so authorised staff can disarm the system before the alarm triggers.

What happens if someone smashes the window at night?

A glass-break detector can be added where the survey identifies a need. Photo confirmation covers the area viewed by the selected MotionCam detector. Optional monitoring follows the agreed alert and response arrangements.

What if staff are threatened during trading hours?

A discreet panic button under the counter lets staff call for help without drawing attention. With PRO Monitoring, the alert reaches our team and our response partner at once, first responders attend, and serious incidents are escalated to armed response.

Will installation disrupt trading?

We confirm installation time after the survey and agree a schedule around your trading hours.
