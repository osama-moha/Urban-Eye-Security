The Security Gap

Cameras fail when they are placed randomly and monitored poorly.

Having cameras is not the same as having security. Standard CCTV records video to a DVR in a back office. When an intruder enters, nobody is watching. You get footage of the theft the next morning, but the assets are already gone. Without AI filtering, night visibility, and direct response integration, standard cameras merely record your losses.
