Before you book.

What equipment does each camera plan include?

The wireless plan lists five cameras; suitable memory cards are separately specified. Wired plans include the listed cameras, recorder, surveillance drive and up to 20 m of Cat6 per camera. Installation and longer or outdoor-rated runs are itemised in the quotation.

How long is footage kept?

Retention depends on camera count, resolution, frame rate, compression, recording schedule and scene activity. We size storage for the agreed target rather than promise a fixed number of days.

Can we view cameras on a phone?

Yes, with compatible equipment, authorised app access and a working connection. Local recording and remote access have different power and connectivity requirements.

Do we need a paid health check?

New installations start with a free survey. The paid CCTV Health Check diagnoses an existing system. We confirm its scope, fee and any credit conditions before booking.
