Existing system support

Existing CCTV System Health Check

If cameras are offline, footage is missing, storage fills up too quickly, night images are poor, or nobody has reliable administrator access, we inspect the existing system before recommending replacements.

What the health check covers

Recording and retention

We check whether cameras are recording, how long footage is likely to be retained, whether storage is healthy, and whether the recorder can handle the current setup.

Coverage and image quality

We look at camera position, blind spots, lighting, night performance, entrances, cash points, stock areas, loading bays, gates, and other risk areas.

Power, cabling, and network

We inspect PoE capacity, cable condition, switch capacity, network load, NVR bandwidth, backup power, and remote viewing reliability.

Administration and handover

We review who has authorised access, how accounts and passwords are managed, whether the recorder is documented, and what is needed for a proper handover.

Verification requirement: Access changes, account recovery, credential resets, and system takeover work are completed only for verified owners or authorised client representatives.

You receive a short written summary of the main faults, immediate risks, recommended next steps, and a quotation if work is needed.

Before you book.

What does the kit price include?

Wireless equipment starts from KES 32,700, with memory cards at KES 4,500 each. Wired kits include the stated equipment and up to 20 m of Cat6 per camera. Installation, outdoor-rated or longer cable runs are separately quoted.

How long is footage kept?

Retention depends on camera count, resolution, frame rate, compression, recording schedule and scene activity. We size storage for the agreed target rather than promise a fixed number of days.

Can we view cameras on a phone?

Yes, with compatible equipment, authorised app access and a working connection. Local recording and remote access have different power and connectivity requirements.

Do we need a paid health check?

New installations start with a free survey. The paid CCTV Health Check diagnoses an existing system. Its price, scope and credit conditions are shown above.
