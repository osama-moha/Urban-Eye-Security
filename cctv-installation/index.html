Cameras and privacy

Cameras record people, so we set them up with care.

When a business records customers or staff, Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 applies. These three habits keep your system useful and fair.

Cover your property, not your neighbours

Cameras should watch your gates, doors, and premises, not neighbouring homes, and never private spaces such as bathrooms or changing rooms.

Tell people they are being recorded

Put clear signs at entrances, and tell staff where cameras are and why they are there.

Keep footage only as long as you need it

Recordings overwrite automatically on a set cycle, and only the people who need footage should be able to open it.

This is general guidance, not legal advice. Businesses with larger camera systems should confirm their obligations with a data protection adviser. Read our privacy-first security guide →

Technical proof

The technology behind the footage.

Our starting kits use TP-Link VIGI cameras and recorders, with TP-Link Tapo for the wireless kit and TRASSIR for advanced analytics on larger sites.

Full Colour After Dark

VIGI full-colour cameras combine a high-sensitivity sensor with built-in supplemental lights to record colour detail at night, up to 30 metres away.

People and Vehicle Detection

Cameras classify people and vehicles, so alerts come from the things you care about rather than every moving shadow.

More Footage on the Same Drive

H.265+ compression stores clear video in less space, which is how a 2TB or 4TB drive holds weeks of recording.

One Cable per Camera

Power over Ethernet runs power and video to each camera over a single cable from the recorder, so there is no separate power supply at every camera.

Works With Your Ajax Alarm

Cameras can be added to an Ajax alarm system so you see live video in the same app as your alarm. Where monitoring operators need camera images with an alarm, we set that up during the survey.

Is CCTV right for you?

A good fit, or better served by something else.

Cameras show you what happened. Sometimes you need something that stops it happening first.

CCTV is a good fit if

  • You need to know who came and went, and when.
  • You want evidence when stock, cash, or property goes missing.
  • You want to check on your home or business from your phone.
  • Your old cameras record grey, blurry footage at night.

You are better served elsewhere if

  • You want an alert before someone gets inside. See Smart Alarm Systems
  • You need to control who can open doors. See Access Control
  • You need someone watching alerts at night. See PRO Monitoring
  • You only want a single camera with no recorder or installation. A kit is more than you need.

What is included in the kit price?

The kit price covers the cameras, recorder, hard drive or memory cards, and cabling materials. Installation labour, conduit and trunking, and any network add-ons are quoted after the free site survey, so your written quotation shows the full cost before anything is installed.

How much footage will I have?

A 4-camera system with a 2TB drive, or an 8-camera system with a 4TB drive, typically keeps around 30 days of continuous recording using H.265+ compression. The exact figure depends on camera settings and how busy the scene is, and recording only when there is motion extends it.

Can I watch the cameras on my phone?

Yes. Wired systems use the free TP-Link VIGI app for live view and playback, and the wireless kit uses the TP-Link Tapo app.

Is there a monthly fee?

Not for recording. Footage is stored on a drive or memory cards on your property. Optional extras, such as 24/7 PRO Monitoring, have their own monthly fees.

Can Urban Eye work with my existing cameras or DVR?

Often, yes. We check which cabling, mounting points, and cameras are still usable before recommending anything new, and replace the weakest points first rather than starting from zero.

How long does installation take?

Most home and small business installations are completed within 1 to 3 days after the site survey and quotation are approved. Larger sites are scheduled in phases.

Do I need 24/7 monitoring with my cameras?

No. Cameras work as a standalone recording and alert system. PRO Monitoring, from KES 8,050/month, adds operators who verify alerts and coordinate the response, instead of footage you only review after the fact.
