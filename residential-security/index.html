Which home is yours?

Protection works best when it matches how your home is lived in.

A standalone compound and a fifth-floor apartment face different risks. Find yours below to see what usually fits.

Standalone homes and compounds

A long boundary wall, a large compound, and a gate guard who cannot see every side.

Outdoor detectors watch walls, verandas, and driveways, so an intruder is flagged before reaching a door or window, and photo detectors show you what triggered the alert.

Usually fits: Home Complete

Apartments

The main door is the main target during working hours, when nobody is home.

A door sensor sends a push notification the moment the door opens, and a motion detector confirms whether someone is actually inside.

Usually fits: Home Essential

Family homes while you travel

You are away for days, and your only protection is a gate guard or a neighbour.

Arm and disarm from your phone, see a photo of whatever triggers the alarm, and decide what to do from wherever you are.

Usually fits: Home Verified

Airbnb and short-stay units

Keys get copied, guests leave doors unlocked, and indoor cameras are not an option.

Door sensors, keypad codes you can change between guests, and emergency alerts protect the unit without watching your guests.

Depending on the building, shared infrastructure can serve several homes, with each household controlling only its own unit from the app.

The Urban Eye Solution

The Three Lines of Protection.

We do not place random sensors. Each system is built to deter, detect, and delay intruders before they reach your family, with clear mobile alerts at every stage.

First Line: Outdoor Protection

Curtain and outdoor detectors draw narrow detection lines along walls, verandas, and balconies, so an intruder is flagged before reaching the house. Outdoor lighting and electric fence alerts can be linked to the same app.

Second Line: Building Perimeter

Door and window contacts and glass-break detectors protect the shell of your home: main doors, ground-floor windows, roof access, vents, and balconies.

Third Line: Interior & Assets

Pet-immune sensors and visual verification protect interior rooms, safes, and valuables, so you know exactly what triggered the alarm.

Beyond break-ins

Add protection for fire, floods, and everyday convenience.

These options connect to the same app as your alarm. They are recommended and quoted during your site survey.

Smoke and fire alerts

Wireless fire detectors watch for smoke, rapid heat rise, and carbon monoxide, and alert your phone the moment they trigger.

Leak detection and water shut-off

Leak sensors near tanks, washing machines, and pipes detect water on the floor, and a motorised valve can shut off the water supply automatically.

Security lighting

Outdoor floodlights can switch on automatically when a perimeter detector trips, lighting the compound for your cameras and your neighbours.

Gate and garage control

Open an electric gate or garage door from the same app you use to arm and disarm the alarm.

Electric fence alerts

Link an existing fence energiser so you get an alert on your phone if the fence is cut, shorted, or tampered with.

Power saving when you leave

Arming the alarm can switch off selected appliances, such as water heaters or air conditioners, to cut waste and electrical fire risk.

Is a home alarm right for you?

A good fit, or better served by something else.

We would rather point you to the right protection than sell you the wrong one.

A home alarm is a good fit if

  • You want an alert, and proof, before deciding who to call.
  • Your home has clear entry points: a gate, doors, windows, or balconies.
  • KPLC outages or unreliable Wi-Fi have let a basic alarm down.
  • You want to own your equipment instead of leasing it.

You are better served elsewhere if

  • You want a trained person on your gate day and night. See Manned Guarding
  • You want continuous recorded video of the whole compound. See CCTV Installation
  • Your main risk is a long, exposed boundary wall. See Electric Fencing
  • You only want a siren, with no app or monthly connection. A smart alarm is not the right product.

What does a home alarm system cost?

Home Essential starts at KES 59,800, Home Verified at KES 176,100, and Home Complete at KES 411,800. Each needs Urban Eye Connect from KES 1,500/month to stay online and send alerts. Your final price is confirmed after a site survey.

Can I stop Urban Eye Connect and keep my system?

Yes. You own the hardware outright with no lease obligations, and you can stop the service at any time while keeping your equipment.

Will my pets constantly trigger the alarm?

No. We can use pet-immune sensors designed to ignore the movement of domestic animals while still reacting to human intruders. This allows you to secure your home while pets move freely in selected areas.

Will the system work during a KPLC blackout?

Yes. Hub backup batteries keep the system running for up to 16 hours during a power cut, and dual SIM hubs keep alerts reaching your phone if your Wi-Fi goes down.

Does the Three Lines approach work for apartments too?

Yes, but it is scaled to your needs. For middle-floor apartments, we usually focus on doors, accessible windows, and interior motion. For ground-floor or top-floor apartments, we may also review balconies, roof access, or shared corridors.

Do I need PRO Monitoring for my home?

No. You can receive alerts and act on them yourself at KES 0/month. PRO Monitoring, from KES 8,050/month, suits homes where nobody can reliably respond, for example while you travel.

Do I really need a site survey first?

Yes. Every home is different. A site survey helps identify real blind spots and recommend the right equipment, preventing overspending on unnecessary devices or leaving important access points unprotected.
