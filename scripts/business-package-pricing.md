# Business package pricing policy

Approved 22 October 2026. Target gross margin: 40%. Minimum gross margin for standard installations: 35%. Gross margin is calculated before VAT and is not net profit.

Target selling price = total direct delivery cost / 0.60. Minimum selling price = total direct delivery cost / 0.65. Recalculate margin after any discount.

Use the current supplier quotation or invoice, not a presumed MSRP discount. Include actual freight/import/currency costs only where not already in the supplier price, installation labour, travel, consumables, commissioning, training and an appropriate warranty provision. Establish VAT treatment and avoid double counting. Monthly service costs and margin are calculated separately.

Supplier PDF reference, not confirmed landed or installed costs:
- Business Essential: USD 574.81 installer equipment reference.
- Business Photo: USD 643.19 installer equipment reference.
- Business Outdoor: USD 892.08 installer equipment reference.

These references carry no margin. Do not publish a KES starting price by simply converting these totals. Confirm current availability, regional SKU/firmware compatibility, direct delivery costs and tax treatment first.

Public wording until validated: Quoted after a site survey. Once validated: From KES [amount] installed, with the exact starting equipment and standard installation scope. Show VAT and required monthly charges explicitly. Use a seven-day quotation validity period, subject to confirmed availability, and define payment/acceptance terms in each quotation. Check current costs before issuing quotes; revise affected public starting prices after supplier changes.

The three intrusion offers do not include CCTV recording, Suprema access, fire systems, guarding or operator monitoring. These are separately scoped. No automatic higher Connect tariff for a larger equipment package: justify any service difference by its scope and delivery cost.
